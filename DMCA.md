# DMCA & Copyright Policy

This product is an original creation of Sapra AI. If you believe your copyrighted material is present, please open an issue or reach out to our agent at [support@sapra.app](mailto:support@sapra.app) for immediate resolution/takedown.

## Issue resolution policy

1. **Report**: open a GitHub issue titled `Copyright: <short description>`, or contact the agent above. Include the file/line concerned, a link to or description of your original work, and how to reach you.
2. **Acknowledge**: we aim to acknowledge reports within 3 business days.
3. **Interim action**: where a claim is plausible, the disputed material is removed from the default branch while it is reviewed.
4. **Resolve**: within 14 days of acknowledgement we either confirm removal (including from future releases) or explain why we believe the material is original.
5. **Counter-notice**: contributors whose material was removed may respond through the same channel.

No third-party trademarks are used to name or market this product.
